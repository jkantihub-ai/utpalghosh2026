02 · Flagship Venture · Healthcode Protein Box Pvt. Ltd.

Protein Box — Eat Like A Pro

“You have the will... we have the meal... and we deliver it fresh too.”

The Problem

Fitness centers and health awareness are growing rapidly across Indian cities, yet everyday food awareness and reliable access to freshly prepared, macro-balanced healthy meals remain scarce.

The Solution

Protein Box bridges that gap with freshly cooked, high-protein meals delivered daily—alongside specialized diet plans covering clinical diets, therapeutic meals, senior citizen care, and kids’ healthy meal boxes.

Types of Meals

01

Healthy & Fresh

Fresh Salads · Healthy Lunch Boxes

Daily prepared whole-ingredient bowls and lunch boxes built for clean everyday nutrition.

02

Performance & Recovery

Post-Workout Meals · Pro Athlete Meals

Macro-calibrated nutrition trusted by national and international athletes and bodybuilders.

03

Everyday Essentials

Corporate Meals · Kids Meals · Specialized Diets

Tailored subscriptions for busy professionals, schools, senior care, and clinical or therapeutic needs.

04

Power Shakes & Grills

100% Natural High-Protein Shakes · Grilled Meals

No-added-sugar natural shakes and freshly grilled high-protein platters designed for flavor and fuel.
